# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Avron contributors and maintainers commit to making participation respectful, safe, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback focused on the work.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information or security report without consent.
- Keep discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Deliberately using Avron against systems without authorization is also outside the project's community expectations.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, decline contributions, or restrict participation in response to behavior they consider harmful. They will consider the impact and context of a report and aim to handle it promptly and confidentially.

To report a conduct concern, contact the project maintainers privately through the contact route listed in the repository. For security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting procedure in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the public issue tracker.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. For interpretation, the Covenant text is available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
